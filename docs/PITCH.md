# DocDoor — Pitch Summary

**Turkey's on-demand home-visit doctor platform**
_March 2026_

> Public version of the seed pitch. **Funding figures, valuation, financial projections and named investors are intentionally omitted.**

---

## 1. Problem — Turkey's Hospital Queue Crisis

- **500M+** outpatient visits per year
- **2–4 hours** average waiting time
- **9.6M** people aged 65+ (rising ~20% in 5 years), the segment that struggles most to reach hospitals
- Demand for home healthcare is rising, but there is **no digital solution**

## 2. Solution — "Uber for Doctors"

1. **Share location** — patient enters address and symptoms in the app
2. **Match** — the algorithm finds the nearest available doctor
3. **Home visit** — the doctor examines the patient at home
4. **Secure payment** — card or private insurance

**Differentiator:** no direct competitor operating this model in Turkey. Only own-practice / eligible private physicians are onboarded — never public-sector doctors (regulatory-compliant).

## 3. Market

A large, almost-undigitized home-health market (multi-billion ₺ TAM) concentrated in İstanbul, Ankara and İzmir's digital segment. A structural tailwind: Turkey's 65+ share is projected to roughly double over the coming decades — the group with the highest need and willingness to pay for home care.

## 4. Business Model — Commission-Based, 3 Revenue Streams

- **Session commission (~20%)** — taken on each home visit
- **Corporate packages (B2B)** — monthly health packages for companies (low CAC, high LTV)
- **Subscription (MRR)** — chronic-care follow-up for diabetes/hypertension patients

## 5. Current Status & Traction

- ✅ **MVP** — fully functional PWA web app
- ✅ **Payments** — iyzico integration (pre-auth + capture)
- ✅ **Insurance** — private-insurance eligibility check (national-ID based)
- ✅ **Admin** — comprehensive admin panel
- ✅ **Legal** — KVKK-compliant, regulation-aware design
- 🔄 **Doctors** — first cohort onboarding

## 6. Competitive Advantage

- **First-mover** — empty market; an early network becomes defensible
- **Network effects** — more doctors → faster service → more patients → more doctors
- **Data moat** — every session generates demand/seasonality/outcome data a competitor can't copy
- **Legal readiness** — KVKK-compliant, mandatory malpractice; a 12+ month head start on infrastructure

## 7. Growth Strategy (5 phases, 60 months)

Survival (first doctors & PMF) → early traction (ops team, raise prep) → growth (multi-city + telehealth) → maturity (capacity solution, path to profitability) → scale (regional/exit options).

## 8. Risks & Mitigations (transparent)

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|:----------:|------------|
| Regulatory ban | ~35% | Counsel from day 1, ministry dialogue, telehealth pivot |
| Doctor supply crisis | ~50% | Guaranteed income, malpractice insurance, fast payouts |
| Cash runway | ~40% | Bridge ready, cost-cut plan, reserve rule |
| Big-player entry | ~25% | Speed + exclusive doctor contracts |
| Data breach (KVKK) | ~20% | VERBİS, E2E encryption, pen-tests |
| Capacity bottleneck | ~90% | Telehealth + full-time doctor pilot |

> We're honest: this is a high-risk venture — but the risk-management plan is ready.

## 9. Team (key roles)

CEO/Founder · CTO · Medical Advisor · Operations Manager · Growth Lead · Legal Counsel.

## 10. Why Now?

Turkey is aging fast — a structural, demographic opportunity. Home consultation is shifting from luxury to necessity, and the first platform to scale captures the network effect.
